The Blood Rites

One Blood Alchemon goes down alone. Four rooms each hold a captive of a classical element. Solve the room, its two ingredients meet, and the captive's blood runs home to the Circle. Then the Heart takes Blood, and the four it freed have to make Blood again. At the boss, all four fight beside you.

How each room plays

The Circle. Tap a ring to turn it an eighth. A freed captive's blood runs from its door into its cup. Where both rings have a groove at a door, the blood runs past the cup into the middle instead.

Earth. Drag from a root in the wall to lead its tendril. Drag back to pull it in. Tendrils never share a square. Tap a plate's iron hub to turn it a quarter. A plate won't turn while a tendril lies on it.

Water. Tap the floor to walk Blood there (or use the arrow keys). FLIP turns the room over, and the faint outlines show where everything will land. Ice that rests against fire melts, and water puts fire out.

Fire. Swipe or tap beside Blood to step. The twin takes the mirrored step. A plate holds its gate open, on either side, while someone stands on it.

Air. Swipe or tap beside Blood to push off. You drift until something stops you. Push again against ice to send the ice drifting.

The Heart. Tap one of the four, then tap where it should go. Two on an altar's stones fuse, and what they make shoots its power down that altar's lane. Whatever it breaks lets go of the element inside, which joins you. The split stage takes a fused creature apart. Light and Dark make Blood.

Proved. Every room was solved by a search before it was drawn. Earth works with exactly one setting of its plates. Water needs at least three flips. Fire is impossible without its plates. Air takes nineteen pushes. The Heart takes nine fusions and two splits, and can't be done without the split stage.
