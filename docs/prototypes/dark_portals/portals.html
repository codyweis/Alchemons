Nythralor · Dark's descent · prototype

The Black Sun

Each Dark carries one portal. Light shines through them. Through one dark it stays light. Through both, it comes out as blood, and blood is solid.

How it works
Tap floor to walk the chosen creature there. Tap a creature to choose it. The others stay exactly where you left them. Dark: casts portal end I or II straight ahead. Only obsidian (black glass) takes it. Cast an end onto the face it already holds to close it. Dark's body drinks light. A beam stops dead at it. Light: shines only from the burning-glass in the floor. Its body lets light through. Walk into a portal to come out of the other end. Beams go through too. Blood: a beam through both Darks' portals turns red. White seals want white, red seals want blood. Blood over the void is a bridge.
